# Bhowmik, Grimmelt (2026): The exceptional set of the Goldbach problem (survey)

## Citation/link

G. Bhowmik, L. Grimmelt, "The exceptional set of the Goldbach problem", arXiv:2607.27282 (v2, 13 Aug 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27282

## What it says

- An expository survey of the bounds on E(X) = #{even N <= X not a sum of two primes}: Hardy-Littlewood (on GRH), then van der Corput, Chudakov and Estermann (1937-38, unconditional E(X) = o(X)), Montgomery-Vaughan (1975, X^{1-δ}) and Pintz (δ = 0.28).
- Section 3 (pp. 10-13) explains the basic mechanism. Parseval gives a mean-square bound Σ_{N<=X} |r_2(N) - N S(N)|^2. Chebyshev's inequality and S(2N') >> 1 then show that r_2(N) >= N S(N)/2 for all but few even N.
- Section 4 says that Siegel-Walfisz (moduli q <= (log X)^A) handles the major arcs and Vinogradov's estimate handles the minor arcs.
- The pages I read do not mention a variant with one prime restricted to a small range, or the difference problem h = q - p.

## Relevance (C013, hypothesis (G))

This is the standard "Parseval + Chebyshev" scheme that, adapted, proves (G). It is background only: it does not state (G).
