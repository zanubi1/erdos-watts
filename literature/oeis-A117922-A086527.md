# OEIS A117922, A117925, A086527, A282700, A128280, A051237

## Citation/link

All entries were read as raw files from the oeisdata GitHub mirror, e.g. https://raw.githubusercontent.com/oeis/oeisdata/main/seq/A117/A117922.seq. oeis.org itself was not tried directly this pass; it failed DNS in earlier passes.

## What it says (read 2026-11-25)

- **A117922** (Zumkeller 2006, edited by Hasler 2015): "Inverse of A055265, assuming it is a permutation, as conjectured." The entry has a b-file (Noe, n <= 1000), a Haskell program and cross-references to A117924 / A117925. **No comments, no formula, and no bound on A117922(n) - n.**
- **A117925**: fixed points of A055265. It has programs only, with no theory.
- **A086527** (Murthy 2003): the primes a(n) + a(n-1). Comment: "Not every prime appears." The first missing odd primes are 197, 199, 317, 467, 563, 599, 653, 811, 829, 863, 977 (Noe 2011). Formula: a(n) = A055265(n) + A055265(n+1). No proofs.
- **A282700** (Sloane 2017): primes missing from A086527. It has the name and cross-references only.
- **A128280** (Seidov 2007; Hasler): the a(0)=0 / a(1)=2 variant. Comments: "may well be a rearrangement of natural numbers", and A128280 = A055265 ± 1 by parity (Hasler 2020). It points to the Hasler wiki and the A329333 ff. variants. No proofs.
- **A051237** (lexicographically earliest prime pyramid): it appears that the rows converge to A055265. One comment says that, assuming Dickson's conjecture (or Hardy-Littlewood Conjecture B), no backtracking is needed. That is a conditional remark about the pyramid, not about A055265's permutation property.

## Relevance

- **C010 / delay bounds**: no OEIS entry around A055265 states or proves any bound on A117922(n) - n (the lifetime of n). The only quantitative data are the empirical displacement records in A282696/A282697 (see oeis-A055265.md).
- **L009**: no "payer"-type counting appears in any of these entries.
- The A086527 list of missing primes (197, 199, ...) is a curiosity related to the prime-partner structure. It does not bear on L009 or C010 directly.
