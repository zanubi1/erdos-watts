# Sloane, "The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences" (2018)

## Citation/link

N. J. A. Sloane, "The On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences", arXiv:1805.10343 [math.CO], 25 May 2018. The arXiv version says "To appear in Notices of the AMS". The published version is believed to be Notices AMS 65(9), 2018, but that reference was not checked.

## What it says (read 2026-11-25, PDF pp. 1-12)

- Section 5 discusses the EKG and Yellowstone sequences, with a full proof that Yellowstone is a permutation (Theorem 2, the same six steps as in the Yellowstone paper).
- **Section 6, "Three further lexicographically earliest sequences"**: A127202 (proved), A280864 ("almost" a proof) and **A055265**. Sloane says all three are surely permutations, but the third "may be beyond reach". On A055265 specifically: terms appear to lie on or near a(n) = n, "but the proof that every number appears may be difficult because it involves the gaps between the primes."

## Relevance

- Confirms that, as of 2018, the leading OEIS editor regarded the permutation property of A055265 as open and probably hard. He named prime gaps as the obstacle, which is consistent with our C006 / C010 reductions to prime counts in short intervals.
- It contains no lemma of the C009 type and no delay bound.
