# M. F. Hasler, "Prime sums from neighboring terms" (OEIS wiki)

## Citation/link

https://oeis.org/wiki/User:M._F._Hasler/Prime_sums_from_neighboring_terms (created Nov 2019, later updated). Origin: Éric Angelini's SeqFans posts, Nov 2019.

## What it says (from search snippets only)

- Family of sequences: among the pairwise sums of any M consecutive terms, (at least / exactly) N are prime. A055265 is the case (M, N) = (2, 1); A055266 is (2, 0); A329411, A329574, etc. are other cases.
- Parity bound: at most floor(M/2)·ceil(M/2) of the pairwise sums can be prime.
- Some cases would require/imply the twin prime conjecture or analogues.
- The members are *conjectured* to be permutations. One search summary claimed the page says there is "no formal proof" but offers a heuristic; a second, more targeted search could **not** confirm that wording. Treat as unverified.
